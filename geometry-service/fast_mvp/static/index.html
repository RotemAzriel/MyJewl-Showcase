Start From a Photo

Upload a ring photo as a starting point, then confirm the stone details, ring size, and metal — those are always typed, never read from pixels. Photo matching against our validated catalog is a separate lane that is currently paused, so your picture is not used to choose the mounting.

Real-Time 3D Preview

See your design materialize as you tune parameters. Interactive viewport with drag rotation, metal switching, and live dimension updates.

Constraint Validation
